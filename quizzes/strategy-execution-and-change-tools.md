  - id: 4
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A product strategy is clear and well documented, but engineers and sales staff have no protected time, authority, incentives, or confidence to act. Which tool best identifies the situation as clear direction with weak energy and participation?"
    options:
      - "Cummings and Wilson: Orientation and Animation"
      - "Michaud and Thoenig's Strategic Orientation"
      - "Greiner's Growth and Crisis Model"
      - "Porter's Five Forces"
    answer: "Cummings and Wilson: Orientation and Animation"
    explanation: "Cummings and Wilson distinguish Orientation, which is clarity of direction, from Animation, which is motivation, participation, capacity, and action. Clear direction with low animation is a planning trap. Michaud and Thoenig instead classify organizational posture using perceived pressure and response horizon."

  - id: 6

    question: "A fast-growing product team has delegated substantial authority to customer teams. It now finds that teams promise different service levels, create incompatible workflows, and duplicate work. Which Greiner crisis is most likely active?"
    options:
      - "Crisis of control"
      - "Crisis of leadership"
      - "Crisis of autonomy"

    answer: "Crisis of control"
