  - id: 3
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A firm has a promising autonomous product idea, but customer willingness to pay, repeatable delivery economics, and data feasibility remain unproven. The firm has enough cash to fund small experiments without endangering core operations. Which position in Burgelman and Grove's Strategy Bet Model applies?"
    options:
      - "Safe bet"
      - "Wait to bet"
      - "Bet the company"
      - "Desperate bet"
    answer: "Wait to bet"
    explanation: "Wait to bet applies when cash reserves are sufficient but the opportunity is not yet validated. The firm should run bounded experiments and gather evidence rather than scale prematurely. A Safe Bet requires both validation and sufficient cash."

    answer: "Kim and Mauborgne's Four Actions Framework"
    explanation: "The Four Actions Framework uses Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, and Create to redesign the value curve. Porter's Generic Strategies can define a broad differentiation position, but ERRC specifies how to redesign the offering and its cost-value logic."
