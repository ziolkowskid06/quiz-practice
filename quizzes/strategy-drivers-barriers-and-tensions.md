      - "Porter's Generic Strategies"

    answer: "Lewin's Force Field Analysis"
    explanation: "Lewin's Force Field Analysis maps driving and restraining forces around a desired change. It helps identify which constraints can be reduced or which drivers can be strengthened.     options:
      - "Crisis of leadership"
      - "Crisis of autonomy"
      - "Crisis of control"
      - "Crisis of red tape"
    answer: "Crisis of red tape"
    explanation: "In Greiner's model, growth through coordination can eventually create a crisis of red tape. The next response is more effective collaboration and simpler controls."
