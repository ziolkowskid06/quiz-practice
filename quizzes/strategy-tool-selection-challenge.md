  - id: 1
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A manufacturer has falling margins and suspects that price pressure, supplier concentration, substitutes, buyer power, and new entrants may all be contributing. It needs an industry-level diagnosis before choosing a response. Which tool should it use first?"
    options:
      - "Porter's Five Forces"
      - "SWOT Analysis"
      - "Balanced Scorecard"
      - "Kotter's Eight Phases of Change"
    answer: "Porter's Five Forces"
    explanation: "Porter's Five Forces diagnoses the structural sources of industry profitability: rivalry, buyer power, supplier power, threat of substitutes, and threat of new entrants. SWOT can summarize internal and external findings later, but it does not provide the same industry-structure analysis."

  - id: 2
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "After interviews, a company has a long list of internal strengths and weaknesses plus external opportunities and threats. It wants to synthesize the findings and identify strategic options that use strengths to capture opportunities or reduce exposure to threats. Which tool is the best fit?"
    options:
      - "SWOT and TOWS"
      - "Porter's Five Forces"
      - "BCG Growth-Share Matrix"
      - "Lewin's Force Field Analysis"
    answer: "SWOT and TOWS"
    explanation: "SWOT organizes internal Strengths and Weaknesses with external Opportunities and Threats. TOWS turns that diagnosis into strategic options such as using strengths to pursue opportunities, using strengths to counter threats, improving weaknesses to capture opportunities, or reducing vulnerabilities."

  - id: 3
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "An established industrial-services company is considering selling its current services to a new customer segment, while using essentially the same capability base. Which Ansoff growth route is it considering?"
    options:
      - "Market Penetration"
      - "Market Development"
      - "Product Development"
      - "Diversification"
    answer: "Market Development"
    explanation: "Market Development means taking existing products or services into new markets, segments, regions, or customer groups. Market Penetration sells existing offerings to existing markets; Product Development creates new offerings for existing markets; Diversification is new offerings in new markets."

  - id: 4
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A leadership team wants to create an uncontested market space by removing costly features customers do not value, reducing over-served elements, raising important benefits, and creating new sources of value. Which tool is the best fit?"
    options:
      - "Kim and Mauborgne's Four Actions Framework"
      - "Porter's Generic Strategies"
      - "McKinsey 7-S Framework"
      - "Hrebiniak's Model of Strategy Execution"
    answer: "Kim and Mauborgne's Four Actions Framework"
    explanation: "The Four Actions Framework asks what to Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, and Create in order to reconstruct buyer value and cost. It is designed for value innovation and Blue Ocean-style strategic moves, whereas Porter's Generic Strategies positions a firm within existing competitive scope and advantage."

  - id: 5
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A company has agreed its strategic direction, but the strategy is not happening consistently. Leaders need to check whether strategy, structure, systems, shared values, style, staff, and skills reinforce one another. Which tool should they use?"
    options:
      - "McKinsey 7-S Framework"
      - "Porter's Five Forces"
      - "Ansoff's Growth Grid"
      - "Scenario Planning"
    answer: "McKinsey 7-S Framework"
    explanation: "McKinsey 7-S diagnoses organizational alignment across Strategy, Structure, Systems, Shared Values, Style, Staff, and Skills. It is especially useful when a sound strategy is undermined by misalignment in the organization that must execute it."

  - id: 6
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A company is deciding whether to double down on a high-growth, low-share software offer. It needs to understand whether the offer has validated customer value and whether the firm has enough cash to absorb the downside during scaling. Which tool is the best fit?"
    options:
      - "Burgelman and Grove's Strategy Bet Model"
      - "BCG Growth-Share Matrix"
      - "Lewin's Force Field Analysis"
      - "Kotter's Eight Phases of Change"
    answer: "Burgelman and Grove's Strategy Bet Model"
    explanation: "The Strategy Bet Model assesses opportunity validation and cash reserves, helping leaders distinguish among bets that are safe, strategic, foolish, or dead. BCG identifies relative market share and market growth, but it does not directly assess validated value or financial capacity to survive the bet."

  - id: 7
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "A company is debating whether to offer a fully standardized digital service worldwide or adapt the service and commercial model for local regulations and customer practices. It needs to make the trade-off explicit rather than assume one side is always correct. Which tool is the best fit?"
    options:
      - "De Wit and Meyer's Strategy Tensions"
      - "Balanced Scorecard"
      - "BCG Growth-Share Matrix"
      - "Porter's Value Chain and Value System"
    answer: "De Wit and Meyer's Strategy Tensions"
    explanation: "This is a Responsiveness versus Synergy tension. De Wit and Meyer helps leaders decide whether to choose, sequence, separate, or synthesize competing values such as local adaptation and cross-unit standardization."

  - id: 8
    type: multiple-choice
    question: "An executive team must decide what to do when a possible regulatory change, a technology breakthrough, and a customer-budget downturn could combine in several plausible ways over the next three years. It needs to prepare robust options rather than forecast one future. Which tool should it use?"
    options:
      - "Scenario Planning"
      - "SWOT Analysis"
      - "Value Chain Analysis"
      - "Balanced Scorecard"
    answer: "Scenario Planning"
    explanation: "Scenario Planning explores a small number of plausible, materially different futures built around critical uncertainties. It helps leaders identify early indicators, robust actions, contingent moves, and strategic options. SWOT summarizes current conditions, rather than exploring alternative future environments."

    options:
      - "Five Forces analyzes industry structure and the sources of competitive pressure"
      - "SWOT combines internal and external findings in one matrix"
      - "Five Forces directly identifies an organization's internal skills gap"
      - "SWOT can be used to synthesize findings from Five Forces"
      - "Five Forces includes buyer power and supplier power"

    answers:
      - "Five Forces analyzes industry structure and the sources of competitive pressure"
      - "SWOT combines internal and external findings in one matrix"
      - "SWOT can be used to synthesize findings from Five Forces"

    explanation: "Five Forces is an external industry-structure analysis. SWOT combines internal and external diagnosis, and can use Five Forces findings as inputs to Opportunities and Threats. Neither tool automatically selects the strategy; leaders must make choices from the analysis."

  - id: 12
    type: select-all

    options:
      - "Make explicit choices about priorities, scope, and trade-offs"
      - "Define a small set of strategic objectives and outcome measures"
      - "Identify capabilities, ownership, decision rights, incentives, and dependencies"
      - "Assess driving forces, restraints, and stakeholder readiness for change"
      - "Assume the diagnosis itself will change behavior without communication or action"
      - "Collect every possible metric before deciding anything"
    answers:
      - "Make explicit choices about priorities, scope, and trade-offs"
      - "Define a small set of strategic objectives and outcome measures"
      - "Identify capabilities, ownership, decision rights, incentives, and dependencies"
      - "Assess driving forces, restraints, and stakeholder readiness for change"
    explanation: "Good strategy moves from diagnosis to choices, a measurable design, and an executable change approach.   - id: 14
    type: fill-blank
    question: "In Porter's Five Forces, the ability of customers to demand lower prices, better terms, or higher service levels is buyer ___."
    answer: "power"
    accept:
      - "buyer power"
    explanation: "Buyer power rises when customers are concentrated, switching costs are low, offerings are undifferentiated, information is available, or buyers can credibly integrate backward. It can compress profitability even in a growing market."
